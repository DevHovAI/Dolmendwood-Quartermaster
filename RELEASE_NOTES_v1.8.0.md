The catalogue grows to hold what the books hold, the hex number does the Referee's bookkeeping, and the players get a bar of their own.

## Everything the books put a price on

**273 new catalogue entries**, in thirteen categories of their own rather than one heap:

- **Common Fungi and Herbs** (Player's Book p130) — the twenty species a herbalist sells, with the book's own effect line and availability.
- **Treasures and Oddments** (Campaign Book) — amulets and talismans, magic balms and oils, crystals, garments, rings, potions, wondrous items, enchanted oddments, rare comestibles, rare fungi, rare herbs, and the magic armour and weapons the d100 tables price.

Weights are the books' own encumbrance notes, not guesses. Every figure was rebuilt into the book's own line and checked against its page.

**Treasures are found, not bought.** Everything imported is marked as not for sale, so no shop stocks a Bag of Holding by accident. Where you *want* a particular alchemist to sell a particular potion, the new **From Catalogue** button puts it on that shop's shelf by hand — folded categories, a search, and a tick that takes a whole category at once.

## Type the hex, and the book answers

The day bar has a **hex box**. Type the number the party is standing in and the terrain, its Travel Point cost, the region and the chance of losing the way all follow from the Campaign Book — **195 hexes**, with the hex's name, anything the book notes about encounters there, and a button that opens its page.

**Fifty-three of those hexes grow something the foraging tables know nothing about**, and a successful forage now rolls that too: *"this hex as well: 2 × Sage Toe (1d3)"*, with the name linking to the page that says what it does.

## A day bar for the players

Optional, and carrying only what the characters would know: what the party is doing, Travel Points walked, the weather once it has been rolled, and each player's own hunger, rest and the penalty they add up to. The duty list, the hex, the region and every page reference stay with the Referee.

The module's windows can be reached from the bar instead of the toolbar, if you would rather have the left-hand controls back.

## Smaller things

- **Per-entry delete in the trash** — throw one row away for good without emptying the bin.
- **The day counter and the world clock now move each other**: the clock passing midnight advances the day as before, and the bar's own ▶ pushes the clock to 7:00 the next morning.
- Page references on the cards for the fishing, foraging and hunting tables, which had none.
- Fixed: a shop or inn name containing an apostrophe no longer breaks the map-note form.
- Fixed: an inn saved before rations were added shows them without needing its Extras reset.
- Fixed: the hex typed on the bar was thrown away on every read, so nothing downstream ever saw it.

The module is now called **Dolmendudes Companion** — it has not been only an inventory manager for a while.

**No text, tables or artwork from the Dolmenwood books ship with this module.** What ships is the reference; the page it points at is in your own copy.
